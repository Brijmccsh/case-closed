How it works

From ruling to real life in four steps.

Every case in Case Closed follows the same path, so you always know where you are and what comes next.

  1. Step 01

    Read the TL;DR

    Start with a two-line summary of what happened and what the court decided. No legal background needed.

    TL;DR
  2. Step 02

    Decode the ruling

    Tap highlighted legal terms for plain-English definitions. Each case breaks down what happened, what the court decided, what it means for you, and how to know your rights, with the original source linked.

    What happenedWhat the court decidedWhat it means for youKnow your rights
  3. Step 03

    Test yourself + cast a verdict

    Take a quick three-question check. Then vote on the ruling. You'll only see how others voted after you cast your own.

    ✓✓3 Results unlock after you vote
  4. Step 04

    Write a Case with Coach

    Build your own brief: Facts, Legal question, Holding, Reasoning, Why it matters. The Coach asks guiding questions and never hands over the answer.
